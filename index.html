Service Hierarchy
Why L1 and L2?
TRANSACTIONS_DETAILS.ITEM_DESCR is a free-text POS field — a mix of SKU codes, standardized names, part numbers, and technician notes. A two-level hierarchy makes this usable at both executive and operational levels.
L1 · Category Level
15 broad service categories for executive reporting and KPI tracking.
Answers: "Does this store offer brake services?"
Examples: Brakes, Tires, Battery, Engine, Transmission, Fluids & Cooling
L2 · Subcategory Level
80 operational subcategories for drill-down analysis.
Answers: "Does this store do front brake pads vs. rotors vs. brake fluid?"
Examples: Brake Pads — Front, Rotors — Rear, Brake Fluid, Brake Labor
Example: How "26BB PREMIUM WIPER BLADE" gets classified
ITEM_DESCR = "26BB PREMIUM WIPER BLADE" → SQL CASE checks: BB PREMIUM WIPER matches → L1: Wiper Blades → L2: Front Wiper Blades
Classification Logic
How ITEM_DESCR Is Matched
SQL CASE/WHEN on UPPER(ITEM_DESCR) — first matching rule wins
L1 CategoryKey Match PatternsNotes
BrakesBRAKE PAD ROTOR CALIPER DOT 3/4Front/Rear split by FRONT/REAR prefix. Transaction-level oil exclusion: if any line is oil, entire transaction removed.
TiresTIRE ROTATION TPMS ALIGNMENT TIRE MOUNTReplacement tracked via !REPLACED N TIRE checklist codes from POS.
BatteryDHGOL AGM PLAT DIEHARD GOLD ^T[0-9]+ GOLDMost battery replacement rows are DieHard SKU codes, not plain text. Regex catches the part number format.
Air Filters^A[0-9]{4} ^C[0-9]{5} AIR FILTERWix/Fram part# formats: A#### = engine air filter, C##### = cabin air filter.
